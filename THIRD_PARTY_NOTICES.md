## LiquidGlass by Yassine Bouane

The interactive Refraction Lab uses `@ybouane/liquidglass` to render real-time WebGL refraction, chromatic aberration, Fresnel reflection, specular lighting, bevel depth, and blur over a captured DOM scene.

Source: https://github.com/ybouane/liquidglass
Package: https://www.jsdelivr.com/package/npm/@ybouane/liquidglass
Pinned version used by the site: 1.0.3
License: MIT

The library is loaded from jsDelivr at runtime. The site keeps a CSS-only visual fallback for cases where WebGL or the module cannot initialize, but the primary Glass Lab effect is the WebGL renderer.
